Your footage, pictures and sound are never changed: every media file in a project's assets/ is an original, kept once more beside the project and read-only on disk, and Studio puts back one that changed or went missing outside it. Agents in the app now ask before they edit or run a command, and the app answers for them: their own work in the project goes ahead, an original or anything outside the project is refused.
